## 3. How Dynamic Syncing Works

1. **Instant Loading (Stale-While-Revalidate)**:
   - When visitors open your site, products load instantly from local browser storage so the site is super fast.
   - In the background, the site fetches live data directly from Google Sheets.
2. **Instant Manual Sync**:
   - At the top of the Chemical Catalog, there is a green **"Live Google Sheet Syncing Active"** banner.
   - Clicking **"Sync Now"** immediately re-fetches the latest values from your Google Sheet.
3. **Changing Google Sheet URL**:
   - If you ever switch to a new Google Sheet, click **"Sheet Settings"** in the green banner on your website.
   - Paste the new Published CSV URL and click **Save & Sync Now**.

---

## 4. How to Publish a New Google Sheet to Web

If you create a new spreadsheet in Google Sheets:
1. Open the Google Sheet.
2. Click **File > Share > Publish to web**.
3. Under *Link*, select the sheet tab containing products and set format to **Comma-separated values (.csv)**.
4. Click **Publish**.
5. Copy the link generated by Google Sheets and paste it into **Sheet Settings** on the website.
